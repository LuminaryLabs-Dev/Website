Client Features

Selected highlights from our work with clients, showcasing real-world impact and collaboration.

Dancing With The Ancestors

Dancing with the Ancestors was a unique PhD project by Sultan Sharrief, presented at the Black Bar Social at the LA Music Center earlier this year. We collaborated with Sultan over the course of a year to bring this captivating experience to life, with a significant focus on crafting high-quality, magical visual effects. Our primary role was to design and implement advanced VFX and shaders that brought the Depthkit volumetric clips to life, ensuring they stood out as stunning focal points in the production. Additionally, we built custom environments for each character, amplifying the immersive storytelling with complementary visual elements. This project was a true showcase of how powerful and dynamic visual effects can elevate virtual productions, and we’re proud to have contributed to its success.

Museum Multiverse

Museum Multiverse is a novel 3rrd person VR experience originally developed for GearVR, our team brought this game to the next level by bringing it to Meta Quest. The game involves exploring different realms, solving straightforward puzzles, and most importantly walking through a vivid story of hope and personal affirmation.

Oticon Hearing Tech

Oticon is an audio-first VR experience. Working closely with a sound engineer, we developed a demo to showcase the features of Oticon hearing aids in comparison with their competitors. Built-in Unity with the VR Interaction Framework, the Oticon experience offers this experience through real-time immersion on Quest lineup devices. The purpose of the project was to promote Oticon’s brand as a solution for providing affordable hearing aids to Veterans.

Pillow Sleep App

Pillow is a published immersive app designed to be used lying down, and Crimson, our CTO, worked extensively on its development. The experience lets users relax in bed in mixed reality, exploring four interactive dreams—stargazing, fishing, meditating, and playing interactive bedtime stories—each with unique mechanics and co-op play. Built for the Quest, Pillow is the only app of its kind, offering a new way to unwind, learn, and connect with friends in a social MR environment.
