APEX Benchworks is the hardware-services bench within Luminary Labs, focused on desktop PCs, gaming systems, custom builds, and high-value home computers.

Purpose

Make the machine you have work the way you need it to.

We combine careful hardware work with transparent parts selection, BIOS configuration, and testing tied to the outcome you actually asked for.

The work

A clear path from the problem to a tested result.

Each job moves through a defined bench process so scope, approvals, and results stay understandable.

  1. 01

    Describe the machine

    Send the specifications you know, photos, symptoms, your budget, and the result you want.

    Specs · photos · symptoms · goals
  2. 02

    Plan and quote the work

    Known upgrades receive an upfront parts BOM. Unknown failures begin with diagnosis before replacement parts are approved.

    Compatibility · BOM · labor · approval
  3. 03

    Local handoff or shipping

    Local jobs can be arranged for handoff. Approved mail-in jobs receive a shipping plan appropriate to the machine.

    Handoff · packaging · intake documentation
  4. 04

    Benchwork

    We complete the approved repair, upgrade, rebuild, cooling work, customization, or component installation.

    Hardware · cooling · lighting · assembly
  5. 05

    Configure and validate

    Relevant BIOS, firmware, thermals, memory, storage, graphics, networking, and stability checks are performed around the job.

    BIOS · stress testing · thermals · benchmarks
  6. 06

    Return with results

    The machine comes back with a concise record of the approved work and the validation performed.

    Work summary · validation · pickup or return
Capabilities

Specific work for specific machines.

Jobs can combine categories when a machine needs more than one kind of work.

Scroll through the bench capabilities.

01 / Repair

Hardware Repair

Replace failed or damaged components when repairing the existing machine makes sense.

  • GPU, PSU, RAM, motherboard, and storage
  • Fans, wireless hardware, and cooling
  • Case components and tempered glass
02 / Upgrade

Performance Upgrades

Choose compatible components around a target workload, budget, resolution, or capacity.

  • GPU, CPU, RAM, and storage
  • Power-supply and platform planning
  • Wireless and expansion hardware
03 / Rebuild

System Rebuilds

Reuse good hardware while changing the case, platform, layout, or overall assembly.

  • Case swaps and platform changes
  • Full disassembly and reassembly
  • Cable routing and organization
04 / Cooling

Cooling & Airflow

Improve cooling around the case, component heat load, and acceptable noise level.

  • Fan layouts and airflow correction
  • AIO and water-cooling installation
  • Thermal servicing and fan curves
05 / Customize

Lighting & Customization

Clean hardware-focused visual work for a system that should look as intentional as it performs.

  • RGB and ARGB systems
  • Controllers and fan hubs
  • Case parts, glass, and cable cleanup
06 / Configure

BIOS & Configuration

Configure the installed hardware correctly without positioning APEX as general-purpose IT support.

  • BIOS / UEFI and firmware updates
  • XMP / EXPO, boot, fan, and power settings
  • Linux installation or Windows hardware setup
Transparent by Design

Known upgrades can be quoted upfront. Unknown failures begin with a diagnostic and move to a repair BOM after inspection.
