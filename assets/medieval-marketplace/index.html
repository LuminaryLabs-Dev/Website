A closer look at the source collection

Build a market from the details up.

The 66 draft models cover market stalls and displays, carts, barrels, crates, produce, signs, furniture, and environmental dressing. The source archive also contains editable Blender files and individual render previews.

The free sampler is a small, clearly labeled test set—not the entire draft collection. Geometry polish, pivots, scale, target-engine imports and commercial rights for the complete product remain open for review.

Common questions

Can I use these in a commercial game?

No commercial license is granted by this free preview. It is supplied for private evaluation; commercial usage and redistribution require separately approved terms.

Is Unity or Unreal compatibility guaranteed?

No. These are source GLB drafts. Unity and Unreal imports and performance have not been tested or certified.

Does this include all 66 models?

No. The download contains four representative models. The 66-model archive remains an internal draft until reviewed for release.

Where is Stripe checkout?

The payment integration is not configured yet. The only available download here is free and does not use checkout.
