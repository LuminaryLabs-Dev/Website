Project Arrowhead / Agora

Project Arrowhead is a revolutionary XR marketplace and MDM-Lite service designed for the Steam Frame ecosystem. The platform connects vendors and end-users through a unified storefront, content management layer, and enterprise-grade device orchestration — positioning Luminary Labs at the center of the emerging XR hardware marketplace.

🎯 Platform Overview

XR Marketplace

MDM-Lite

Lightweight mobile device management for enterprise XR fleet orchestration
