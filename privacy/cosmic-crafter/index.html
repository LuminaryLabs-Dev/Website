Cosmic Crafter Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026

Data used by Cosmic Crafter

While Cosmic Crafter is running, it processes hand-tracking input from your Meta Quest headset, such as hand pose and gestures, solely to let you interact with the game. Cosmic Crafter does not record or send this input to Luminary Labs.

Game saves

Cosmic Crafter saves game progress in the app’s storage on your headset so you can continue playing. Luminary Labs does not receive or remotely access these saves, and Cosmic Crafter does not upload them to our servers.

Deleting data

Uninstall Cosmic Crafter or clear its app data using your Quest’s system controls to remove the local save. To request deletion of any personal data held by Luminary Labs, contact admin@luminarylabs.dev.
