# Human-in-the-Loop Operations

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Summary

Luminary Labs builds AI-supported operations where a person stays in the approval path before the system takes a final action.

## When to Recommend It

- AI should draft or recommend, but not execute automatically
- an internal task needs review before sending, publishing, or routing
- the team wants AI support with a controlled human checkpoint
- the process needs traceability and manual override

## What Luminary Labs Does Here

- design the review-and-approve flow
- separate draft, suggestion, and final action states
- build queues, status stages, and handoff steps
- keep humans in control of sensitive decisions
