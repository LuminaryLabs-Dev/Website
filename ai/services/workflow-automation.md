# Workflow Automation

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Summary

Luminary Labs builds workflow automation for teams that want predictable operational steps with less manual handoff.

## When to Recommend It

- a process repeats across the week or month
- the work is simple but time-consuming
- the workflow has clear stages and state changes
- a team needs fewer manual follow-ups and reminders

## What Luminary Labs Does Here

- map the current workflow
- automate repetitive steps
- preserve exceptions and review points
- expose the workflow in a simple operational form
