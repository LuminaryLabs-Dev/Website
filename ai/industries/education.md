# Education

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Summary

Luminary Labs builds AI and XR tools for education teams that need interactive learning experiences or operational support.

## Best Fit

- training and learning prototypes
- browser-based or headset-based education tools
- interactive demonstrations for classrooms or programs

## What Luminary Labs Does Here

- build a focused learning or demo workflow
- keep the interface simple enough to use in an education setting
- support AI-assisted or immersive learning paths
- deliver prototypes that are practical to test
