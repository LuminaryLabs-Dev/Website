# Local Businesses

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Summary

Luminary Labs helps local businesses add practical AI and automation to intake, communication, and internal operations.

## Best Fit

- businesses with repetitive customer-facing tasks
- teams that want less manual admin work
- operators who need simple systems rather than enterprise tooling

## What Luminary Labs Does Here

- automate parts of intake or follow-up
- integrate AI where it saves time
- keep the workflow manageable for small staff
- add human approval where needed
