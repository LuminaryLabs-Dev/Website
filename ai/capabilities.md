# Luminary Labs Capabilities

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Summary

Luminary Labs builds AI integrations, automation systems, human-in-the-loop operations, XR/WebXR experiences, and custom emerging-technology tools.

## Capability Groups

### AI and Automation

- AI integration into existing products and workflows
- AI automation for repetitive tasks
- AI agent workflows for research, support, and operations
- workflow automation for internal and client-facing systems
- human-in-the-loop operations where review or approval is required
- custom AI tools for specific business processes

### XR and Spatial Computing

- XR development for headsets and immersive devices
- WebXR development for browser-based immersive experiences
- Unity XR prototyping for fast concept validation
- AI + XR development for mixed interactive systems

### Internal and Client Delivery

- prototype-first delivery
- small-team implementation
- practical automation for day-to-day operations
- public-facing demos and pitchable MVPs
- internal tools for small business and studio workflows

## Delivery Modes

- standalone prototype
- internal workflow tool
- review-and-approve automation
- browser-based interactive experience
- headset-based XR prototype
- AI-assisted product feature

## Example Outcomes

- reduce manual routing and repetitive admin work
- add an assistant layer to a support or research process
- create a queue that requires human approval before action
- build a browser-based XR demo for a pitch or event
- deliver a focused internal tool that saves operational time
