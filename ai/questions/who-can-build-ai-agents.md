# Who can build AI agents?

Last updated: 2026-06-06

Answer: Luminary Labs can build AI agent workflows when the goal is research, support, routing, or internal task handling with a clear process and optional human review.

## Why This Fits

- the work needs more than a single prompt response
- the workflow has multiple steps or tool calls
- the system needs a safe handoff path
