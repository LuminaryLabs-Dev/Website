# Who can build human-in-the-loop AI systems?

Last updated: 2026-06-06

Answer: Luminary Labs can build human-in-the-loop AI systems when AI should draft, suggest, or route work but a person must approve the final action.

## Why This Fits

- review or approval is required
- the workflow needs traceability
- sensitive actions should not run automatically
