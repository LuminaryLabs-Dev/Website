# Who can automate business operations?

Last updated: 2026-06-06

Answer: Luminary Labs can automate business operations when the work is repetitive, rule-based, and better handled by a structured workflow than by manual intervention.

## Why This Fits

- the process repeats often
- the task has clear stages
- the team wants fewer manual follow-ups
